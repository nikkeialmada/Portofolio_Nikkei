  backgroundObjectives: |
    The brief was to take a laterite nickel resource through to a
    feasibility-level mine plan and an economic verdict.

    Objectives:

    - Derive optimal pit layouts from the resource model.
    - Build a mine schedule that feeds a steady product stream.
    - Test the plan against hydrological constraints and project economics.
  keyFeaturesCoreLogic: |
    ### Pit optimisation to design

    Economic pit limits were generated from the block model, then hand-refined
    into practical pit, ramp, and dump layouts.

    ### Schedule tied to targets

    The mining schedule was built to hold product grade and volume within
    feasibility targets across the life of mine.

    ### Water and money as constraints, not afterthoughts

    Hydrological analysis informed pit and dump placement; a techno-economic
    model confirmed the plan's viability.
