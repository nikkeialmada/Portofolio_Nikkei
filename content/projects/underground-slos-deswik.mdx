  backgroundObjectives: |
    The deposit's geometry and grade suited an underground operation on the
    sub-level stoping method. The task was a feasibility-level design and a
    defensible production schedule.

    Objectives:

    - Lay out development and stopes for a sub-level stoping operation.
    - Build a production schedule that sustains feasibility-level output over the mine life.
    - Prepare the reclamation section of the feasibility study report.
  keyFeaturesCoreLogic: |
    ### Layout driven by the method

    Access, level intervals, and stope geometry follow sub-level stoping practice
    and the shape of the orebody, so the design is buildable rather than only
    optimal on paper.

    ### Scheduling in Deswik

    The production schedule was built in **Deswik**: development and stoping
    activities were given dependencies and sequenced against equipment and grade
    targets, then checked for practical continuity. The walkthrough above steps
    through that schedule.

    ### Closure planned from the start

    The reclamation section sets out how disturbed surface and underground areas
    are handled after mining, as part of the same feasibility report.
