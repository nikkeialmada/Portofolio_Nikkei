  summary: >
    Designed and delivered a structured, three-day Deswik.CAD course for Aksara
    Karir — taking participants from first click in the interface to a

    mine planning engineers upskilling.
  backgroundObjectives: |
    Deswik.CAD is a standard tool for nickel mine planning, but most newcomers
    only get a shallow, self-taught introduction to it. Aksara Karir asked for an
    instructor with real field experience to design a course that goes beyond
    button-by-button tutorials.

    Objectives:

    - Give first-time users a clear path from the interface to a working pit design.
    - Cover the full planning workflow: block model, pit/dump design, haul roads, and reporting.
    - Make the material usable immediately in day-to-day mine planning work, not just theory.
  keyFeaturesCoreLogic: |
    ### Day 1 — Foundations

    Deswik.CAD interface, basic point/polyline tools, and core design tools
    (project, offset, surface, solid), ending with a simple pit design built
    from scratch.

    ### Day 2 — Planning with the block model

    Working with the block model inside Deswik, designing a pit against the ore
    model with the pit/dump design module, and building a waste dump design.

    ### Day 3 — Infrastructure & delivery

    Haul-road design, plan-to-actual surface reconciliation, catchment analysis
    for water management, and section/layout plotting with orthophoto overlay
    for a presentation-ready output.

    Delivered live over Zoom across three evenings, with an e-certificate,
    session recordings, and e-materials for every participant.
