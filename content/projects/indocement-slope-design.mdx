    Feasibility study reports for four material types at a cement operation each
    needed defensible pit-slope angles. I ran the geotechnical analysis behind
    those slopes and the mining recommendations that came with them.
  backgroundObjectives: |
    Life-of-Mine designs for limestone, claystone, laterite, and silica at the
    Tarjun site each needed slope heights and angles that were safe without being
    overly conservative.

    Objectives:

    - Determine optimal single-slope geometry per material from geotechnical analysis.
    - Feed those slopes into the Life-of-Mine mine design.
    - Deliver the geotechnical section of the feasibility study reports.
  keyFeaturesCoreLogic: |
    ### A slope per material, not one number for the pit

    Each material was analysed on its own — limestone, claystone, laterite,
    silica — because their strength and behaviour differ.

    ### From core to recommendation

    Drilling data was interpreted, slope-stability analysis run, and the results
    turned into specific mining recommendations.

    ### Balancing safety and strip

    Slope height and angle were chosen to hold stability while avoiding
    unnecessary waste stripping over the mine life.
