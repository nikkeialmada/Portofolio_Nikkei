  summary: >
    The reporting behind GEORIVAL needed data from many formats to line up and
    stay current. This pipeline ingests exports and imagery, automates the
    grade–tonnage report, and builds section profiles from large point clouds.
  backgroundObjectives: |
    Reserve and grade–tonnage reporting depended on data that arrived in
    different industry formats and had to be reconciled by hand.

    Objectives:

    - Integrate industry-standard data exports and historical satellite imagery into one workflow.
    - Automate grade–tonnage reporting so it can be re-run on demand.
    - Produce accurate section profiles from large-scale point-cloud data.
  keyFeaturesCoreLogic: |
    ### One ingest path for many formats

    Exports and imagery are normalised into a common structure before anything
    is computed.

    ### Reporting that re-runs itself

    Grade–tonnage figures are generated by the pipeline rather than assembled by
    hand, so an update is a re-run, not a rebuild.

    ### Point clouds at scale

    A processing step handles 80,000+ points to cut section profiles that match
    the underlying survey.
