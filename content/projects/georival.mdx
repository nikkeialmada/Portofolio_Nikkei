caseStudy:
  summary: >
    Field teams on a nickel laterite site still moved paper maps and spreadsheets
    between the pit and the office. GEORIVAL puts the block model, drill holes,
    and reserve figures on an Android device so planning decisions can be made
    and checked on the ground.
  backgroundObjectives: |
    Exploration and short-term planning data lived in disconnected desktop files,
    so anything checked in the field meant a round trip to the office.

    Objectives:

    - Put 2D block-model visualisation, drill-hole data, and GeoPDF maps in one Android app.
    - Reproduce the operation's reserve reporting exactly — no "close enough" numbers.
    - Give field staff simple productivity tools tied to the same data.
  keyFeaturesCoreLogic: |
    ### Reserve reporting to mining-engineering standard

    The app implements the operation's own reserve logic — domain-specific
    **Recovery** and **Dilution**, **cut-off grade** classification, and
    **Stripping Ratio** — and the output was validated against real operational
    data before it was trusted.

    ### One data model, many views

    Block model, drill holes, and GeoPDF layers are read from a single structure,
    so the map, the sections, and the grade–tonnage summary always agree.

    ### Built for the pit, not the desk

    2D block-model rendering and drill-hole browsing are designed to stay usable
    on a phone screen in the field.
